About UniNest

Student accommodation made easier to find

UniNest helps students discover accommodation options around Cape Town student areas, compare useful details, and connect with the right next step.

Explore Listings Contact Support

Our purpose

Finding accommodation should not feel scattered or confusing. UniNest brings listing discovery, student support, and landlord access into one place so the search feels more organised.

For Students

Browse accommodation by location, room type, budget, and facilities.

For Landlords

Use the portal to manage properties, messages, and booking requests.

For Cape Town Areas

Focus on student-friendly areas such as Rondebosch, Bellville, and the CBD.

How UniNest works

1

Search

Students browse accommodation options by area and basic needs.

2

Compare

Listings show useful details such as price, room type, and facilities.

3

Connect

Students can move from listing discovery to contact or booking support.

4

Manage

Landlords and admins can use the portal for property management.

Need support from UniNest?

Our contact page is available for student questions, landlord support, and help with accommodation details.
